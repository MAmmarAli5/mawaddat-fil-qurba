ABOUT THE PLATFORM

A Digital Space for Islamic Learning

Mawaddat-e-Fil-Qurba is an online educational initiative created to provide students with an organized and accessible environment for Islamic studies.

Our academic programs focus on understanding the teachings of the Holy Quran and the intellectual, spiritual and practical heritage of Ahlul Bayt (A.S.).
