Mawaddat-e-Fil-Qurba

Mawaddat-e-Fil-Qurba is an online Islamic educational initiative created to provide students with an organized, accessible and meaningful environment for Islamic studies.

Our academic focus is on understanding the teachings of the Holy Qur'an and the intellectual, spiritual and practical heritage of Ahlul Bayt (A.S.).
